# Vocals, guitar, drums, mix

Ignore format badges at first. In Add Module, tap a **job chip** (Vocals, Guitar, Drums, Mix) or search. Then drop the tile in the right place ([Channel Rack vs mixer inserts](rack-vs-mixer.md)).

## Vocals

1. Record: red **Record** → **Playlist tape (mic)**, or Sampler **Record Audio**
2. Route the take / rack row with **Trk** to a mixer strip
3. Strip **FX** → **Equalizer** (tone) → later **Compressor** → **Reverb** or **Delay**

Keep MX **Apply** off unless you want the FX baked into the WAV.

## Guitar

1. Load the DI or amp take into Sampler, or park it on a Rack row
2. Mixer **FX** → **Filter** or **Equalizer** for tone
3. **Distort** for grit; **Chorus** or **Delay** after, not before
4. **Reverb** last, mix low

## Drums

1. Chop in Sampler → **To Rack**, or add **Click Kit** from Instruments
2. Paint steps on the Channel Rack
3. **Trk** each row to its own insert if you want separate FX
4. **Compressor** or **Limiter** on those strips or on Master

## Mix

- **Equalizer** and **Stereo Shaper** on groups / Master
- **Limiter** last on Master
- One live FX slot per strip — choose the job for that row, don’t stack everything on one sound

## Search shortcuts

| Type | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Vocals | `reverb`, `comp`, `delay`, Space / Dynamics |
| Guitar | `distort`, `chorus`, `filter`, Tone / Modulation |
| Drums | `click`, `comp`, `limit`, Percussion / Dynamics |
| Mix | `eq`, `stereo`, `limit`, Utility / Tone |
